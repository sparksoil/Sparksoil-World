# Look Again — learn to notice

*For beginners, for foggy days, for 12-year-olds. The parts do the sorting so you don't have to.*

You don't need noticing skills. You just answer tiny questions, and the sorting happens by itself — there is no second step where you decide what anything means.

~5 minutes. One sentence is plenty. You can stop after any line.

---

**One small moment, in one sentence:**

_What happened? Just the facts, like a photo caption._


**Now the parts step in. Circle or tap what fits — no explaining needed.**

🌙 Lucy — body:
Did your body notice anything?
tight / light / tired / buzzy / warm / nothing

🦦 Otter — delight:
Was there anything interesting, funny, or nice — even a tiny bit?
yes → ___ / not really

🪶 Pelican — remembering:
Does this remind you of anything? Has it happened before?
yes → ___ / no / not sure

_(That's the whole sort. Whatever got circled is what's alive. Nothing to manage.)_

**What the parts noticed:**

_Read back what's circled. That's it. That's what's alive right now._

_One line if you want, or nothing:_


---

_there you are._
