# Look Again — hold-me version

*Private. For you, not for performance. Not a product, not a scorecard.*

Jehovah already sees what's alive in you — before you have perfect words for it.

Use this when you want a place to notice. ~5 minutes, or less. You can stop after any line. Nothing needs to happen next.
